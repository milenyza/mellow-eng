# Learning analytics

`/meu-progresso` loads on demand. The page reads the current progress snapshot, favorite IDs and annotation metadata. The overview and reports use the small A1 catalog index, shared story and sentence topic metadata, and simulation summaries. Full educational content is loaded by its own routes or through the existing Notebook recommendation service when the learner opens suggestions. The selectors in `services/learning-analytics.ts` derive daily activity, period aggregates, distinct completions and a timestamped milestone list. The visualization selectors consume those same records and can be used with a future backend adapter. They store no new records.

The A1 journey shows published completion (completed published lessons over published lessons), full curriculum completion (completed published lessons over all outlines), unit completion (completed over published in that unit), and publication coverage (published over all outlines). Unit titles in both languages and lesson objectives come from the catalog index. Planned content is shown separately and never counts as completed. Curriculum completion is a measure of activity in Mellow and carries no formal certification.

## Active time and dates

The Dashboard and My Progress use the same canonical time sources. The general daily ledger already includes both lessons and reading, so adding individual run or story time to that ledger would double-count. Games, simulations and flashcards contribute their stored daily buckets once per valid session ID. A day counts once when its combined recorded duration is positive. Historical keys that are not valid calendar days do not contribute.

Stored day keys use the device's local calendar at recording time. The shared clock splits intervals at local midnight. Periods use inclusive dates and allow open ends. Time is filtered by stored day keys. Session counts use the device-local date of completion. First-response metrics use the date of the first submission. A session can contribute time on one day and count as completed on another. Existing Dashboard weeks begin on Monday.

All-time lesson/reading distribution subtracts lifetime story time from the shared ledger if that result can also account for the retained lesson runs. Inconsistent snapshots leave the total and daily buckets unchanged. The Dashboard reports unavailable reading time in that case. A date-filtered breakdown keeps lessons and reading combined because the daily ledger does not identify how much came from either source. Study time is an estimate using visibility, focus and the shared inactivity window.

## Compatibility and historical limitations

The existing version 5 format still accepts versions 1–4. Analytics never rewrites the envelope, backfills timestamps or generates example activity. Sessions that were interrupted are included only according to the previously existing recording rules. Lessons and reading can retain active time while in progress. Games, simulations and flashcards contribute only completed sessions. The older shared daily ledger may have time without a retained run.

One first-completion snapshot and the current run are retained for lessons and stories. Earlier replays, exercise attempts or chapter dates may have been replaced. Run snapshots with the same ID are counted once. Chapter completion totals use lifetime IDs, while recent marks only show actual stored timestamps. A later repeat mark can be the only date available. The recent list shows up to five retained milestones in the selected period. The calendar shows dated milestones for the selected day. Missing dates and unsaved historical performances remain unknown.

Session records and current personal choices remain local to this browser. A time zone change can alter how old completion timestamps map to a date. Stored time buckets keep their original date. Similar metrics across modules retain their separate meanings and are never combined into accuracy or mastery scores.

## Time navigation and visualizations

- Presets include the current Monday–Sunday week through today, the last 7 or 30 calendar days including today, and the current month through today. All activity is the default. Custom ranges use inclusive bounds with at least one boundary through today. The calendar browses months independently. Completion totals, the curriculum and current vocabulary familiarity are independent of the selected period.
- The chart uses daily buckets for spans of up to 14 days, Monday-based weekly buckets for 15–90 days and calendar-month buckets from 91 days. Partial buckets stop at the selected dates. All activity uses the span between the first and last days with time. With a single custom boundary, the other is the first available day or today. When spans exceed 730 days, the chart lists months with recorded time and labels the omission of empty months. The duration for each point is shown as text. Bars begin at zero and share the same linear scale within a chart. The average divides estimated active time by days with positive recorded time.
- The calendar keeps fixed ranges across months. Cream means zero recorded time, lavender means a positive duration below 5 minutes, sage means 5 to under 15 minutes, and plum means 15 minutes or more. Dashed cells label future dates in the current month. The full date and recorded duration are available in the day button and the selected-day detail. Days with an explicit dated completion can show it even when the time bucket is empty. Daily views end on the device's current date. A future-dated bucket from a clock or time zone change stays in storage and can enter these views when that date arrives. The Dashboard lifetime time metric uses all valid stored days.
- Distribution widths use unrounded time shares. The legend uses whole percentages by flooring exact shares and assigning remaining percentage points by largest fractional remainder, with category order breaking ties. A positive share that rounds to zero is labeled below 1%. An all-activity split is shown only when it reconciles with the displayed shared ledger. If a future-dated shared bucket is excluded or the ledger and lifetime records disagree, lessons and reading stay combined. Filtered breakdowns keep the daily ledger combined.
- An observation names the activity with the most recorded time when one category leads. A comparison is shown for an explicitly bounded period only when positive recorded duration exists in both it and the immediately preceding span of the same number of calendar days. These are comparisons of available records. Coverage can vary by period.

The page uses native buttons, a semantic HTML calendar table and CSS bars inside the existing lazy-loaded route. This keeps exact values available as text, supports keyboard and touch interaction and avoids an extra runtime dependency. The bars use length for time comparison, while the calendar uses fixed intensity ranges and explicit date labels. Charts provide point inspection through buttons, and the calendar uses arrow keys for date focus within the month.

## Module reports

The view opens one report at a time. Selectors in `services/learning-reports.ts` combine the existing analytics projection with lightweight content references. The A1 and story catalogs remain the sources of publication coverage. Canonical sentences and their topics are keyed by the authored challenge ID and shared with the game. Story covers stay in the story module. The Notebook suggestion disclosure lazy-loads the existing component and uses the same recommendation service and evidence as the Notebook.

| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Lessons             | Distinct completion, current in-progress and available lessons, and the unit journey.                   | Each dated submission, exercises with a correct submission, explicit correction reviews, and first submissions by mode. Suggested lesson topics use the authored lesson focus for a retained incorrect submission or reviewed correction.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Stories             | Completed stories, current readings, distinct lifetime chapter marks, and catalog publication coverage. | Retained comprehension submissions and results, including chapter-level results when a published chapter is identifiable. Lesson and comprehension accuracy counts the first submission of each retained exercise run if it falls in the selected period. An exercise counts as independently answered when at least one correct submission falls in the period. Explicit reviews count by their review timestamp even when the related submission falls outside the range. Lesson topics describe the focus of linked lessons. The historical records support an association with the lesson, while finer attribution would require objective-level exercise metadata. A removed catalog item can contribute to historical aggregate counts while its report has no supported educational link.

Games, simulations and flashcard outcome reports use the local day of session completion because individual response dates are unavailable in those records. Their active durations use the daily time buckets, so time can appear in a period without a session completion. Lessons and reading use the reconciled all-activity estimates when available. The shared daily ledger is shown as a combined category when the individual duration is unavailable in a range.

## Unified activity history

`/meu-progresso/historico` is loaded on demand. `services/activity-history.ts` derives the same dated entries used by recent activity and the calendar. Identity combines the source kind and its stable content or session key. Ordering is descending by timestamp with a stable identity tie-breaker. The view groups by local date, filters by module and inclusive dates, and paginates 12 entries at a time. Its filters start with all recorded activity and are independent from the My Progress period.

The timeline includes first lesson and story completions, the retained finished lesson replay, the latest retained dated chapter mark, Word Match, Sentence Builder, Memory Game, completed Practical English simulations and completed flashcard reviews. First completion and repeated sessions are tracked independently. Unfinished activities, favorites, proficiency scores and estimates of historical events have no timeline entries. Displayed time comes from the individual retained run or session, with the story duration scoped to its first completed run. Chapter-level time is unavailable. Day headings display calendar dates. The app attaches no clock time to a date-only record. Older chapter marks without a usable date still contribute to accumulated reading totals. Educational destinations use the current publication state. Flashcard and game history links use their retained session IDs even when some vocabulary references have been removed from favorites.
